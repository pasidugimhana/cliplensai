MP4 vs WEBM: Which Video Format Should You Use?

2026-11-02 · ClipLens AI

The short answer

Choose MP4 for maximum compatibility. Choose WEBM when you are publishing video on a website and want efficient, open-format delivery. Both can look excellent; the difference is mostly about where the file will be played.

Compatibility

MP4 plays on almost everything: phones, tablets, TVs, editing software and browsers. WEBM is supported by modern desktop browsers and Android, and newer Safari versions, but some older devices and editors still struggle with it.

Compression and file size

WEBM commonly uses VP9, which can produce smaller files than H.264 at similar quality. MP4 with H.264 encodes quickly on almost every device, while newer MP4 files may use H.265 or AV1 for better efficiency.

Which should you pick?

Sharing a clip with friends, uploading to a social network or editing in an app: MP4.

Embedding a short looping video or screen recording on your own site: WEBM, with an MP4 fallback.

Not sure: MP4.
