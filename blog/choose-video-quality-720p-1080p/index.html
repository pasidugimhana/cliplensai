720p vs 1080p: How to Choose the Right Video Quality

2026-11-06 · ClipLens AI

What the numbers mean

720p is 1280×720 pixels and 1080p is 1920×1080 pixels. More pixels mean more detail, but also larger files and more data to download or stream.

Screen size matters

On a phone screen, the difference between 720p and 1080p is often hard to see. On a laptop, monitor or TV, 1080p looks noticeably sharper.

Storage and data

A 1080p video can be roughly twice the size of the same video at 720p. If you are short on storage or using mobile data, 720p or 480p is a sensible choice.

When to keep the original

If you plan to edit the video or archive it, keep the original resolution so you do not lose detail before you start.
