How to Direct Camera Motion Like a Professional Filmmaker

The difference between an average AI video and a breathtaking cinematic masterpiece lies in how you describe camera movement and lighting geometry:

  • FPV Drone Shots: Use terms like "hyper-speed dynamic drone dive, sweeping past obstacles, banking right, motion blur on edges" for intense action sequences.
  • Slow Zoom / Dolly In: Best for emotional character close-ups and dramatic reveals. E.g., "slow cinematic push-in onto the subject's expressive eyes, shallow depth of field, 85mm anamorphic lens".
  • Orbit / 360 Camera Rotation: Creates high-production commercial showcases for futuristic vehicles, architecture, and luxury products.
