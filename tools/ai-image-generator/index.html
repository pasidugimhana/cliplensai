Prompt Engineering: How to Get Masterpiece AI Images

Getting photorealistic results from text-to-image neural models relies on structured prompt composition:

  • Define the Subject Clearly: Specify the main focal object, its posture, emotion, and clothing (e.g., "A thoughtful cyberpunk detective wearing a weathered leather trench coat").
  • Establish the Environment & Mood: Add lighting, atmospheric effects, and background context (e.g., "rain-slicked neon alleyway in Neo-Tokyo, misty haze, cinematic volumetric rim lighting").
  • Detail the Camera & Texture Specs: Keywords like "8k resolution, shot on 35mm lens, f/1.8 aperture, photorealistic textures, trending on ArtStation" guide the model toward professional optical depth of field.
