What Is Seedance AI?

Seedance is a family of AI video generation models developed by ByteDance, the company behind TikTok and CapCut. It turns text prompts and images into short cinematic clips. Seedance 2.0 launched in February 2026 and added multimodal inputs and native audio.

Who Owns Seedance AI?

Seedance is owned and developed by ByteDance. Websites with names like "Tryseedance" or similar are not automatically official, so check who operates a site before paying or logging in.

Is Seedance 2.0 Free? Seedance Pricing

Access depends on the platform. ByteDance's own consumer apps typically offer a limited number of free credits, with paid plans for more, and API access is priced per second of video. Be careful with offers such as "Seedance 2.5 unlimited free trial", "promo" or "free trial 30 days guaranteed": every legitimate provider has finite free credits, so unlimited claims are a red flag. Always confirm pricing on the provider's official pricing page.
