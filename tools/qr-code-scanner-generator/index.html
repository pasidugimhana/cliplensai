How Reed-Solomon Error Correction Protects Your QR Code

Standard QR codes feature built-in Reed-Solomon error correction algorithms. This mathematical redundancy enables a QR code to be scanned accurately even if up to 30% of its surface is smudged, scratched, or covered by a decorative corporate logo. ClipLens applies high-density error correction to ensure maximum readability in low-light environments.
